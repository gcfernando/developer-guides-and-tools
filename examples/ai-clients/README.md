These files are imported personal preference snapshots. They are not a compatibility guarantee for a current client release. Inspect the model, permissions, sandbox and experimental settings, validate against the installed client's official schema, and copy only the settings you intend to use.

Instruction templates are maintained separately under [templates/ai-instructions](../../templates/ai-instructions/README.md). Instructions do not grant permissions or enforce a sandbox.
